Beer With Me is a lighthearted, location-based social app designed around a simple premise: letting your friends know you’re having a drink so they can join you.

Rather than focusing on detailed beer reviews or tasting notes, the app acts as a casual beacon for spontaneous real-life meetups.

🍻 Core Concept & How It Works
Check-In via the "Drink Button":
When you sit down at a pub, bar, or home for a drink, you open the app and tap the main drink button. You select what you’re having—ranging from a standard pint, cider, wine, or cocktails to shots or non-alcoholic options.

Instant Friend Notifications:
Your connected friends receive a push notification letting them know you’ve opened a session.

Interactive Map:
An built-in map displays your current location (or check-in spot) so friends nearby can see where you are and drop by.

Quick Responses:
Friends can send preset reactions or quick messages to let you know if they're on their way to join you or just sending a virtual "Cheers!" from afar.
